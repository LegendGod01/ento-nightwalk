Chapter I

The Gate山門

Every climb begins beneath a threshold. The sanmon does not ask where you have been — only whether you can leave it below. Step through.

Chapter II

Pathways参道

Stone by stone, the mountain rewrites your pace. Lanterns mark the way not to guide your feet, but to slow your breath. The city noise gives way to pine and night air.

Chapter III

Lessons教え

The mountain keeps no schedule. Blossoms fall when they fall. In the dark, a hundred small lights hold their ground — so can you.

Chapter IV

Eternity永遠

Five roofs against the sky, four hundred years of weather, one red moon watching it all. What you climbed for was never the summit — it was the quiet you carried up.
